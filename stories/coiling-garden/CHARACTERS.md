# Characters: Coiling Garden

Quick character sketches for YOLO mode - focused on essentials to get you writing.

## Main Characters

### Mu-Poe

**Role:** Protagonist

**Core Traits:**
- Quietly stubborn — endures indignity without bitterness, but never stops wanting
- Tactile intelligence — understands the world through her hands before her mind
- Improviser — she got into the estate on a lie and keeps it going through competence and nerve

**Background:**
Grew up poor with a mother who sewed and a father who laid brick — both makers, neither artists. She won a regional sculpture prize at sixteen but couldn't afford the tuition that came next. She isn't the maid the Rilutz estate hired. The real maid never showed — Mu-Poe heard about the position, showed up first, and no one checked. In a country at war, people stop asking questions about paperwork. She took the job because it put her inside the house of the sculptor she'd idolized since childhood.

**Primary Goal:**
To become a real sculptor, not just someone who cleans around sculptures.

**Voice Note:**
Speaks sparingly, often in fragments. Thinks in textures and shapes rather than words. When she does talk, it's direct and unpolished — working-class diction that she doesn't bother hiding.

---

### The Serpent

**Role:** Supernatural Presence / Love Interest

**Core Traits:**
- Ancient loneliness — has existed so long that her nature is more atmosphere than personality
- Expressive through touch — communicates in pressure, temperature, and guided motion rather than language
- Possessive tenderness — her affection is real but carries the weight of someone who has turned everything she loved to stone

**Background:**
Not named, not explained. She is the presence in the underground studio — felt but never seen, understood through what she does rather than what she says. Whether she is the Medusa of myth or something older and stranger is never confirmed. Mu-Poe calls her "the serpent" because of the way the air coils when she's near, the way clay spirals under her invisible hands. No journals explain her. No pact is written down. What Mu-Poe learns about her, she learns the way you learn a lover — slowly, through presence, through the shape of what's left behind.

**Primary Goal:**
To be known by someone who chooses to stay.

**Voice Note:**
Never speaks. Communicates through the pressure of her hands on Mu-Poe's, through shapes left in clay, through the temperature of the air shifting warm or cold. Her "dialogue" is sculptural.

---

### Mable Rilutz

**Role:** Shadow / Absent Presence

**Core Traits:**
- Brilliant and ruthless — pursued perfection the way others pursue survival
- Unknowable — Mu-Poe assembles her from traces, not testimony
- Haunting presence — dead, but her choices still shape every room in the estate

**Background:**
World-renowned sculptor who produced works of impossible precision and emotional depth. Critics called her a genius. She died alone in the hedge maze, found with her eyes open and her face frozen in an expression the coroner called "awe." There are no journals. No letters explaining the arrangement. Just the sculptures, the studio that shouldn't exist, and the feeling that Mable Rilutz got exactly what she asked for and couldn't live with it. Mu-Poe pieces together the truth herself — from the sculptures' impossible perfection, from the way the serpent moves, from what the darkness teaches her hands.

**Primary Goal:**
None (deceased) — but her legacy is the architecture Mu-Poe must navigate. Every sculpture in the house is a clue and a warning.

---

### Theo Thregee

**Role:** Antagonist / External Pressure

**Core Traits:**
- Insufferably precise — from his waxed mustache to his itemized ledgers, everything about him is maintained to a fault
- Cultured but transactional — sees art as inventory with market value
- Dogged — once he smells a discrepancy, he won't let it go, and he smells several

**Background:**
Art dealer and executor of Mable Rilutz's estate. Arrived at the mansion with a leather case, a measuring tape, and a mustache that could cut glass — twirled to needle-points, maintained with a devotion that borders on religious. He's been tasked with cataloging and eventually auctioning the collection. The output numbers don't add up — too many works unaccounted for. But his real problem isn't the missing art. It's the maid. Her references don't check out. Her name doesn't match the agency's records. And she wanders the grounds at night with a familiarity that no new hire should have.

**Primary Goal:**
To catalog the estate, find the missing works, and expose whatever fraud is happening under his insufferable mustache.

**Voice Note:**
Speaks in smooth, complete sentences with a faint accent he probably cultivated. Uses art terminology the way a sommelier uses wine terms — technically correct, socially unbearable. Strokes his mustache when thinking, which is always.

## Quick Reference

Characters at a glance:

- **Mu-Poe** - Protagonist, aspiring sculptor / impostor maid
- **The Serpent** - Supernatural presence in the dark studio
- **Mable Rilutz** - Deceased sculptor, absent shadow
- **Theo Thregee** - Art dealer / estate executor, insufferable mustache

## Development Notes

This is a YOLO starter template - lightweight by design.

**As you write:**
- Add details when they emerge naturally
- Characters can surprise you
- Backstory comes when needed
- Relationships develop through scenes

**You can add:**
- Relationships between characters
- Physical descriptions (when relevant)
- Secondary characters as they appear — war refugees, estate staff, townspeople
- Mu-Poe's dog (unnamed — name when it feels right)
- The war as background texture — rationing, distant booms, displaced people

**The YOLO approach:** Discover characters through writing, not planning. These sketches give you a starting point. Let the story tell you who these people really are.
