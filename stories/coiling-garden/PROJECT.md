## Premise

Mu-Poe, an aspiring sculptor who lacked the funds for art school, works as a maid at the mansion of the late world-renowned sculptor Mable Rilutz. When her pet dog runs into the estate's sprawling hedge maze at night, she discovers a hidden underground studio accessible only in darkness — eyes closed or lights out. The master had forged a pact with Medusa, achieving uncanny perfection in her sculptures. What unfolds is a surreal, sensory romance between Mu-Poe and the spirit she can touch but never see — a story told through texture, shadow, and stone.

## Core Themes

- Art and the price of mastery
- Obsession — with craft, with the unseen, with beauty
- Sanctuary in times of war — daily life persisting amid collapse
- Imposture — being someone you're not to become who you are
- The unseen and the untouchable — darkness as intimacy
